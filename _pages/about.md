I am an **External Chair at the University of Strasbourg**, working on advancing **privacy-preserving and trustworthy artificial intelligence**. My research focuses on building AI systems that are not only accurate but also **fair, explainable, and respectful of user privacy**.

I received my Ph.D degree at the Sogang University (South Korea) under the supervision of Seog Park, and worked as a postdoctoral at Sogang University and Inria Saclay(France) under the supervision of Catuscia Palamidessi.

## 🔬 Research Interests

My research lies at the **Trustworthy AI**, with a particular focus on:

- **Federated Learning & Privacy**: Differential privacy, decentralized learning, and privacy-utility trade-offs  
- **Fairness & Trustworthy AI**: Ensuring equitable and robust learning in distributed and personalized settings  
- **Explainable AI (XAI)**: Developing multimodal explanation techniques for complex AI systems, especially document understanding  


## 🤝 Collaboration

I am interested in collaborations related to:

- Privacy-preserving AI  
- Federated and decentralized learning  
- Explainable and trustworthy AI systems  
- AI for document understanding and multimodal data  

Feel free to reach out if you are interested in collaboration or discussion.
